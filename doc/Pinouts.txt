POWER CONNECTIONS
=================

5V RAIL
  |
  +--------------------> LCD Pin 2 (VDD)
  |
  +--------------------> LCD Pin 15 (A / backlight +)
  |
  +--------------------> 10K POT outer pin


3.3V RAIL
  |
  +--------------------> ESP32-S3 3.3V
  |
  +--------------------> GY-521 VCC


COMMON GROUND
  |
  +--------------------> ESP32-S3 GND
  |
  +--------------------> GY-521 GND
  |
  +--------------------> LCD Pin 1 (VSS)
  |
  +--------------------> LCD Pin 5 (R/W)
  |
  +--------------------> LCD Pin 16 (K / backlight -)
  |
  +--------------------> 10K POT outer pin
